Getting to BRYC

Transportation

BRYC provides transportation from most schools to our campuses. Review how it works and the rules, then opt in below. Opt-in closes August 12.

How it works

  • We pick Fellows up from participating schools and bring them to their assigned campus.
  • We do not provide return-home transportation.
  • Routes depend on school location, enrollment, and capacity, and can change during the year.
  • Once you opt in, your Guardian is added to BRYC's texting line (Avochato) for pickup details, changes, and delays.

Is it offered at my school?

Search your school in the schedule tool above to check availability. Availability is set by school and can change based on routes.

Not sure yet? Opt in below anyway. We'll confirm your pickup before the first day, and there's no penalty if a route isn't available.

Transportation rules & expectations

Boarding & pickup +
  • Fellows must be at their designated pickup location on time.
  • There is a 5-minute grace period for boarding.
  • Buses will not return once they depart.
  • By September 4, all Fellows must know their pickup time and location.
  • Missed buses: BRYC will not return for Fellows who miss the bus unless there's an emergency or mechanical issue.
Behavior on the bus or van +
  • Follow driver instructions immediately.
  • Remain seated at all times.
  • Keep all body parts inside the vehicle.
  • Use appropriate volume and language (no profanity or vulgar language).
  • Keep hands to yourself.
  • No eating or drinking.
  • No fighting.
  • Van riders must wear seatbelts at all times.
Communication & attendance +

Family communication

  • Once approved, Guardians are added to BRYC's communication platform.
  • Pickup times and locations are shared through that platform.
  • Schedule changes, delays, and emergencies are communicated promptly.
  • On early-dismissal days, the Director of Operations monitors school calendars and notifies families in advance.

If your Fellow won't ride on a scheduled day

  • Guardians or Fellows must notify BRYC by text or call to the designated staff member, or a message through BRYC's communication platform.
  • Attendance is recorded daily (Operations Associate for buses, driver for vans).
Discipline & three-strike policy +

Disciplinary actions

  • First offense: warning.
  • Second offense: 2-week transportation suspension.
  • Third offense: semester or full-year suspension (determined by the Director of Operations).

Three unexcused absences (no advance notice)

  • Fellow loses transportation privileges.
  • Reinstatement requires BRYC approval.
  • $30 non-refundable reinstatement fee per semester.

Fellow expectations

What we ask of Fellows

  • Uphold BRYC's core values while engaging with BRYC:
HustleIntegrityCommunity
  • Attend at least 80% of weekly programs to remain in good standing.
  • Fellows and Guardians recognize that violating BRYC's core values may result in immediate dismissal. These include, but are not limited to, illicit substance use, fighting, sexual conduct, vandalism, and grievous disrespect of team members, volunteers, partners, or other Fellows.

Building strong relationships with Fellows involves moments of challenge and growth, and we journey alongside them through it.
